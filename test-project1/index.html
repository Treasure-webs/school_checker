Everything you need to know before checking your eligibility.

What are the admission requirements?

You must be at least 16 years old, with a minimum score of 50 in both English and Mathematics. Scores must be between 0 and 100.

Does qualifying mean I have been admitted?

No. This tool checks whether you meet the basic requirements. It does not submit an application or issue an admission offer.

Can I explore more than one course?

Yes. Browse any of our three courses, then click Check eligibility on a course card to select it in the form. The same eligibility rules apply to all three courses.
